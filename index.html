I am a Computer Science student at California State University, San Bernardino with a passion for building software and exploring how complex systems work behind the scenes.

My interests include systems programming, robotics, game technology, and developing tools that solve interesting problems. I enjoy learning by building projects from scratch, experimenting with new ideas, and turning concepts into working software.

Whether I am building small projects, exploring low-level programming concepts, or learning about software and system design, I am motivated by understanding how things work and finding creative ways to create them.

Currently, I am focused on strengthening my programming skills, developing personal projects, and expanding my knowledge of computer science through hands-on experience. This portfolio serves as a collection of my work and a record of my growth as a developer.
